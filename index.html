About me

I am an undergraduate student at South China University of Technology. I work closely with Jinxiu Liu and Yandong Wen on multimodal agent systems for visual and interactive content creation.

My research focuses on agents that translate human intent into controllable outputs across image generation, video generation, 3D/4D generation, visual reasoning, and iterative editing.

I am especially interested in systems that can decompose creative tasks, coordinate specialized models or tools, and improve their outputs through planning, feedback, and self-reflection.

SymGround: Visual Program Synthesis with Symbol-to-Vision Grounding

Yaowen Shi, Jinxiu LiuTech Report

A framework for executable visual artifact generation that turns each revision into a testable symbol-to-vision hypothesis. SymGround uses visual attribution, evidence-guided editing, and edit-render memory to improve slide generation and 3D graphics editing while reducing token usage and invalid executions.
